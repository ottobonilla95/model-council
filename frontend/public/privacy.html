Privacy at Parallax

Updated 3 November 2026.

Your account

When you sign in with Google, Parallax receives your Google account identifier, verified email address and name. These identify your account and link your conversations to you. Google passwords and access tokens are not stored in Parallax.

Your conversations

Messages, responses, research sources and activity are saved in the app’s database, hosted with Neon Postgres. Other users cannot open your conversations through the app. The operator maintains the database and can manage account access.

AI and web research

When you send a message, relevant conversation history and any context you add are sent through OpenRouter to the selected model providers. Research can send queries through OpenRouter’s search service, or Tavily if the operator enables it, and read public websites. Their processing policies also apply. Only submit information you are comfortable sharing with those services.

Browser storage

A secure login cookie keeps you signed in for up to 30 days. Temporary cookies protect the Google sign-in process. Model preferences and optional background context are saved in this browser, separately for each account.

Retention and requests

Conversations remain saved until the operator removes them. To request a copy of your data, removal of your account or other privacy help, contact the person who provided access to this Parallax instance.

Services

Vercel hosts the application. Google provides sign-in. Neon stores account and conversation data. OpenRouter and your selected model providers process AI requests. Parallax uses these services to deliver chat and research.
